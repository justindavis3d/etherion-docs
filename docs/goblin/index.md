# Goblin for Unity

Thank you for buying the **Goblin** character pack! Your support is what lets us keep improving our characters, fixing bugs and building new packs.

This documentation shows you how to get the goblin working in your own game: installing it, connecting it to your AI and animations, giving every goblin its own look, and adding ragdoll deaths and weapons. Use the sidebar to jump to any page, or pick what you want to do below.
